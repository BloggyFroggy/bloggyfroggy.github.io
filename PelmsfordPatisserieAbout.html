All You Could Possibly Want to Know About Pelmsford Patisserie (and then some) - A Brief Summary

Pelmsford Patisserie was founded in July 2008 by Katarina and Lloyd Arlington-Briggs.

In October 2008, we came second place in The Pelmsford Gazette's first ever annual rating of each patisserie and bakery in Pelmsford, and we came second place, closely behind The Pelmsford Bakers. It is an honour to be rated so highly in a village so small.


MEET THE TEAM!

Katarina Arlington-Briggs - Owner/Manager
There from the very beginning, Katarina oversees the patisserie and is the woman behind most of what is here today. She studied food science at the University of Birringford and holds a Level 3 Food Hygiene Certificate, which you can see displayed behind the counter, just above the framed newspaper article about Crete's stray cats.

Stefanie Cresswell - Assistant Manager
With Lloyd's recent departure, Stefanie Cresswell has taken over as assistant manager and she and Katarina run most of the day-to-day operations.

Ffion Williams - Patisserie Assistant
Ffion is the friendly new face you will see behind the counter on most days.

Petros Agelastos - Secretary (and more!)
Petros Agelastos handles all of the administration and marketing, and also is the hardworking hero behind our lovely website.
