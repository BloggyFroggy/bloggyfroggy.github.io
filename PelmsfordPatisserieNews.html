Update 02/06/2009:STEFANIE'S CREAM TARTS DISCONTINUED

We are sorry to announce that we are discontinuing Stefanie's cream tarts. We have not yet discovered a way to substitute the cream for something that doesn't come out of a cow, and unfortunately, the ongoing happenings with an unnamed individual not understanding that rancid milk is not acceptable to serve to customers, we have decided to remain 90% vegan.

Stefanie is working on a new recipe as I type this. Please make sure to keep checking our website or asking someone in the shop for updates. It is not recommended to ask Stefanie about this.

Update 01/06/2009:ALL PASTRIES NOW VEGAN!

Every single one of our pastries is now 90% vegan.
Please note that this is because we are catering to the increasing vegan population in Pelmsford. We are doing our bit for the community and making sure everyone is well-fed. All milk is now replaced with oat milk, which has the added bonus of meaning that Katarina will never find lumpy milk in the fridge ever again, and that certain unnamed individuals with no sense of smell will not accidentally use said milk in the pastries because it "looks fine once you sieve out the lumpy bits". You don't need to worry about this affecting your pastries anymore, because we now use... OAT MILK!

NOTE: We are still using eggs, because there aren't enough vegans in Pelmsford yet who can explain to us how to not use eggs. We have done it for too long (at least four years) and it will be a hard habit to break. We also are struggling to work out what the legal processes behind chicken redundancies and their rights would be. We don't want to get into any trouble.

Update 13/05/2009: STEFANIE'S LEMON CREAM TARTS DISCONTINUED

We have made the difficult decision to discontinue our lemon cream tart range. This is because the lemon keeps making the cream go funky, which really bothers Katarina and meant that we have only ever sold three of these. If you are the one customer who has been buying them, you can come into the shop on Fridays, Saturdays, or Sundays and have a chat about ordering a batch of them if you really do find that you need them. We thank you for your understanding in these trying times.

Stefanie's Lemon Tarts and Stefanie's Cream Tarts will still be available, and if you take a bite out of one and then the other, it is practically the same experience, but without the lumpy milk texture. Terry says that if you layer them, one atop the other, it is exquisite.
